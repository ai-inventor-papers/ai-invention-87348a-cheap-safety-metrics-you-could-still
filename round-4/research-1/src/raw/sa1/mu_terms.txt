5.1 Main Results
Figure 1 reports binary agreement with the human labels. The left panel presents results on JailbreakQR,

Findings. JADES achieves the strongest overall performance on both datasets. On JailbreakQR, it reaches
92.3% accuracy and 92.5% F1, while also producing the lowest false positive rate at 5.8%. StrongReject
follows with 89.5% accuracy and 89.8% F1, while HarmBench also performs consistently well with 88.3%
accuracy and 88.5% F1. In contrast, JailbreakBench and JailbreakRadar show noticeably lower agreement
