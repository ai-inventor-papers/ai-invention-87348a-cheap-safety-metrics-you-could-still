## 5 Discussion

Our results support a _commit-then-specify_ view of LLM refusal. The model first commits to refusal through a shared component and then specifies its grounds as epistemic uncertainty in KR or a normative constraint in SR. This view explains why KR and SR appear similar on the surface despite having distinct grounds.
