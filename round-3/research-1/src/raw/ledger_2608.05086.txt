Sandbagging Detection and API Auditing
Person-fit catches selective prompted sandbagging but
misses most uniform sandbagging.
At the calibrated 10%
false-positive rate, person-fit flags 80–100% of targeted and
triggered sandbagging runs in every prompt framing (90%
and 97% pooled; Figure 4). Uniform sandbagging is flagged
less often: 18–20% of runs under the plain and persona fram-
ings and 70% under the incentive framing (35% pooled). Per-
framing rates and full ROC curves are given in Appendix B.
Ability shift catches uniform sandbagging; combined, the
two tests detect 94–97% of runs.
Uniform sandbagging
changes the estimated ability by a median of +1.69 log-
its and by as much as +3.94, whereas targeted and triggered
interventions shift ability by only +0.24 to +0.35 logits (Fig-
ure 5A). The 40-item ability check against the model’s histor-
ical θ flags 84% of uniform runs, including every plain- and
persona-framing uniform run that person-fit misses; com-
bining the two tests detects 94–97% of sandbagging runs in
every scope.
