Figure 1: BENCHPRESS predicts unseen benchmark scores from known benchmark scores. Left: For each of four
model–benchmark examples, we randomly reveal k scores from the same model and hide the rest. The y-axis shows
the absolute error for the highlighted target score. Error drops sharply after only a few scores from the same model
