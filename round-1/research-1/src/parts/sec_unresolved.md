## J. UNRESOLVED — named, so a later artifact can act on each

Each item below is a specific missing implementation choice, not a general caveat.

**On the incumbent specs**
1. **AMS: the per-model/per-concept selected layer index table** is not printed. The *rule* is fully specified (argmax separation over `range(int(0.4·L), int(0.8·L))`) and codable, so this does not block reimplementation — but the exact indices are unavailable for a same-layer comparison. Also unavailable: the exact refusal-classification string list, and the bootstrap RNG seed.
2. **GFS: the proportionality constant of `w_l ∝ l/L`** is unstated (is it `l/L` or `l/Σl`?), as is the Cohen's d variant (pooled vs per-class SD). The public repo resolves most of the pipeline but its default `--models` flag covers only the 4 core models, not the 21-model pool. **Only 15 of the 21 models could be located by name.**
3. **N-GLARE: the layer-group boundary rule, the slice count `I`, and the per-condition prompt/turn counts** are all unstated. Only 20 of the 40+ models could be located, via an appendix ID-mapping table.
4. **RAS: `|S|`, `|U|`, `|J|`** — the sizes and provenance of the three prompt sets — and the numeric threshold behind "separation gap sufficiently large and stable" for the layer window.
5. **HRCI_repr: whether `C_cos` literally carries absolute-value bars.** PDF-to-text garbled the norm/bar glyphs in Eq 7. We default to the absolute cosine and flag the choice. Also unresolved: the **exact prompt count per carrier** for the harmfulness/refusal direction extraction — located nowhere in the grepped windows, and deliberately not guessed.

**On the candidates**
6. ~~**C2's crossover claim rests on a number we have not read.**~~ **RESOLVED during this artifact.** arXiv:2608.13329's prescription is not a single integer but a four-part design rule — per-prompt scores reported, both arms of the contrast crossed, a content-free control direction scored under the same convention, and "many prompts where current practice uses one" — with their own design crossing 36 wrappers. What remains genuinely open is narrower: whether they anywhere convert that into a minimum n for a *given* target reliability. Our budget claim should be stated against the four-part rule, which is the actionable form.
7. **C5's nearest paper (2608.14929) was verified only at the abstract level.** Its full method was not grepped. It establishes that lineage is recoverable from residual signatures — enough to justify the ablation — but not enough to quote in a methods comparison.
8. **Whether AMS's r = −0.546 would survive our panel at all.** It is n = 14 with a non-significant rank test; the pre-committed response is to report both coefficients, but the power question is genuinely open.

**On the search itself**
9. **The scholarly-mode backend was useless for this field** and every scholarly query is recorded as such. A future artifact that needs peer-reviewed-only coverage should go through ACL Anthology and OpenReview directly rather than through OpenAlex/Crossref.
10. **All three OPEN verdicts survived a deliberate refutation pass (39 queries), and C5 was attacked twice independently.** C3, C4 and C5 were attacked with 15, 12 and 12 query formulations respectively and all held, with C3's and C5's margins narrowed (§F2). C5 additionally received a separate 6-query attack from the coordinator, which converged on the same nearest paper. What remains genuinely untested is not the *existence* of the C5 margin but its *size*: no search establishes how many model-level hidden-state predictors exist that simply never reported an identity control, as opposed to none existing at all.
11. ~~**Three candidates came back OPEN.**~~ Per the plan's own rule, that is weak evidence that queries were too narrow rather than strong evidence that the field is empty. The extra incumbent-vocabulary query per candidate was run (§F), and an independent adversarial pass was commissioned specifically to try to kill C3, C4 and C5 — its result is recorded in `spec_table.json` under each candidate's `adversarial_recheck`. Confidence is set to medium for C3 and C5 and medium-high for C4 accordingly, not to high.
